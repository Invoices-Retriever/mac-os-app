# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is doing their best with the information they
have. Criticise code, not people.

## What we expect

- **Patience with newcomers.** Someone's first plugin is likely also their first
  pull request. The friction they hit is a bug in our tooling or our
  documentation before it is a failing of theirs.
- **Directness without contempt.** "This selector will break on the next
  redesign, here is a sturdier one" is useful. "This is obviously wrong" is not.
- **Respect for the scope.** A feature being turned down is not a judgement of
  the person who suggested it. This project says no to things on purpose, and
  the reasons are written down.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussion. Sexualised language or imagery is not welcome anywhere in the
project.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any
other project space.

## Reporting

Contact a maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) privately.
Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an acknowledgement within a
few days and be told what happens next.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour is inconsistent with this document. Maintainers who break it are held
to it more strictly, not less.
